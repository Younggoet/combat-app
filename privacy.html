COMBAT PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

This explains what Combat collects when you use the app, why, and how you can control it. Combat is operated by William Mayer.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address & passwordTo create and secure your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and never stored by us in plain text.
Display name & team/photo you chooseShown to other users in draft rooms and leaderboards.
Draft picks, room activity, scoresTo run live multiplayer drafts and track your fantasy results.
Basic device/browser infoStandard web request data (like what any site receives) used for debugging and keeping the app working.

We don't collect payment information, government ID, or precise location. We don't sell your data.

2. Cookies & local storage

Combat uses your browser's local storage (not third-party ad trackers) to keep you signed in between visits and to remember in-progress draft state on your device. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings — you'll just need to sign in again.

3. Who else sees your data

Combat runs on two third-party services that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — handles account authentication and stores app data (profiles, rooms, draft picks).
  • Netlify — hosts the Combat website and serves it to your browser.

We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Other users in a draft room can see your display name, team name/photo, and in-room activity — not your email.

4. Your choices

  • Delete your account: email willmaydo1213@gmail.com and we'll remove your account and associated data.
  • Update your info: change your display name or team photo any time from within the app.
  • Opt out of email: account-related emails (confirmation, password reset) are required for a functioning account, but we don't send marketing email.

5. Children

Combat isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach out at willmaydo1213@gmail.com.

This is a plain-language policy written for a small, independent fantasy sports app — not a substitute for legal advice. If Combat expands into regions with specific data-protection laws (like the EU) or adds payment features, this policy should get a legal review.
